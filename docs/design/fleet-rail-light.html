2 · Every state gets exactly one mark

The status resolver already folds eighteen lifecycle and result states into eight tones. The rail renders each tone with one mark, so a row never has to be decoded.

Reserved

no runtime record · starting · resuming · restored

A promise, not a fact, and it looks like one. A worker the backend has not reported on is never allowed to look like a problem.
Running

working · live turn

A 2.4s breathing hairline in the gutter. You feel it when you glance and you do not see it when you look away. One row breathes, always the newest.
Machinery

warm · checkpointing · idle

Uninteresting work should read as almost nothing. Dashed says the machinery is turning, and it costs you no attention at all.
Steered

you sent guidance into a running worker

The mark itself does not change. The title cross-fades to your steer text for 400ms and back. One blink, no toast, proof the orchestrator is still listening.
Yours

waiting · approval or question

The highest budget in the rail, and it is spent once per ask rather than once per row. Only the oldest unmet ask is allowed to pulse.
Settled

completed · blocked · needs delegation · unreadable

A heavy ring, unmistakably more complete than reserved. It dims to 55% after four seconds and then stops. It never leaves, so a finished run is still readable. That is the whole complaint, answered.
Stopped

cancelled

A ring with a gap, and never red. Somebody decided to stop it, so nothing went wrong and nothing in this rail is allowed to imply that it did.
Broken

failed · stalled

The second highest budget, spent once per broken worker. It names the stable failure code and the worker's last legible line, never the raw provider string.

3 · The spine and the census

The spine is derived from the run's own forest entries, one tick per step, capped at nine with an overflow count. The current step is the only slate in the whole rail. The census is exactly four numbers, zeros omitted.

6 · The ambient contract

Six rules, and they are the reason this reads as calm instead of as a second dashboard.

01 · Achromatic at rest

The rail is muted ink on sidebar. No fills, no row borders, no colour until something is genuinely wrong or genuinely yours.

reservedmachinerysettled
02 · One accent, one meaning

Slate means "this is where you are." It is never decoration and it never marks a second row.

current stepfocused
03 · Luminance, not hue

Attention is bought with weight and shape. Exactly three things invert ink: something yours, something broken, and the row you have focused.

quietloud
04 · One loop, two animations

One animation per transition and never a loop, except the newest running worker. Reduced motion collapses all of it to a static mark.

prefers-reduced-motion
05 · Indent, not boxes

A worker is a hairline in the left gutter, one step in per depth. A three-deep tree reads as a tree with zero chrome around it.

06 · Nothing seen is removed

Finished, cancelled, blocked and failed all stay until you dismiss the run. The shape of a run has to outlive the run.
